A useful audit does not stop at a score. It shows the exact evidence, commercial risk, probable source, repair order, owner, and verification method.

PriorityObserved evidenceWhy it mattersRepair and verification
P0Selected size is sold out, but Offer.availability reports InStock.A machine can interpret an unavailable variant as purchasable.Generate availability from the selected child SKU; re-test sold-out and in-stock fixtures.
P0Visible price is £39; structured offer reports 39 USD.The offer describes a materially different commercial fact.Use storefront currency from the pricing source; verify every active locale.
P1Product page says 30-day returns; checkout links to a 14-day policy.The buyer and an automated system receive contradictory terms.Choose one policy owner and synchronize product, FAQ, and checkout surfaces.
P2Product has SKU but no applicable GTIN or MPN explanation.Identity reconciliation may be weaker across feeds and channels.Add real identifiers when they exist; never fabricate one. Document legitimate absence.
