## Evidence Boundary

- Sent messages are **not** qualified leads.
- No reply, submitted request, accepted scope, payment, sale, or revenue has been recorded yet.
- Exact Gmail message identifiers are intentionally not stored in this public repository.
- Both messages are labeled `CommerceLint Outreach` in the connected Gmail account.
- No bulk list, purchased contact data, fabricated defect, artificial urgency, or automated personalization was used.

## Decision Rule

- **Retain and expand carefully** if at least one recipient replies positively or submits a request by the evaluation deadline.
- **Revise** the offer or target segment if replies are neutral but reveal a specific objection or workflow mismatch.
- **Stop this message variant** if both recipients decline or if there is no response after one optional, concise follow-up no earlier than the evaluation deadline.
- Never count a sent message, open, or theoretical opportunity as a lead or revenue.
