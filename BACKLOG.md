# MachineCart Backlog

Priority is impact per unit effort, subject to safety and evidence.

## P0 — Revenue path
1. Connect a transaction-fee-only checkout and verify a complete test purchase path.
2. Instrument landing view, scanner start, scanner completion, offer click, lead, purchase, fee, and refund events.
3. Earn the first qualified visitor through a specific evidence-led teardown or implementation guide.
4. Convert the first founding audit customer and deliver within the promised scope.

## P1 — Distribution and conversion
1. Publish one high-intent platform guide per day while quality remains acceptable.
2. Run a small, manually reviewed agency-pilot outreach cohort using legitimate business contacts only.
3. Add a structured lead form with source attribution and spam controls.
4. Test the $49 founding offer after sufficient qualified exposure; change it only from evidence.
5. Create one public anonymized teardown without inventing a customer or result.

## P2 — Scale and resilience
1. Add automatic analytics ingestion to `METRICS.csv`.
2. Add webhook-driven payment reconciliation and refund accounting.
3. Add regression fixtures for representative product states.
4. Add a second independent health check and alert destination.
5. Review the business weekly and replace the offer after 500 qualified visits with no sales.
