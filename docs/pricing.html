Before you request

How do I pay?

Payment checkout is not yet connected. Request a plan, then agree on the scope and payment arrangement. This website does not collect card information.

When will I get my report?

Delivery timing must be confirmed in the scope response. The report is not generated or delivered instantly when you submit an inquiry.

What if my page cannot be checked?

Unsupported, private or over-limit URLs are rejected before any charge. We need accessible public evidence. If access fails after payment validation, agree on alternative pages or saved HTML, or request the documented remedy. A blocked fetch is not proof your store is defective.

What about cancellations and refunds?

Agree on cancellation and refund terms before payment. If the agreed work cannot be completed, contact pchordia@unsubscriber.me to resolve the scope or request a refund under those terms. This site has no automated refund system.

Are these real customer results?

Example findings are illustrative. No customer testimonial, sales uplift or ranking result is claimed.
