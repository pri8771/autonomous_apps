Sample before you scan everything

Begin with representative product states and templates. A small evidence-rich sample reveals whether the problem is systemic before you spend time crawling the entire catalog.

Separate defects from opportunities

Label incorrect or contradictory commerce facts as defects. Label optional completeness improvements as opportunities. Clients should know which items create immediate risk.

Attach every recommendation to evidence

Include the affected URL, observed value, expected value, customer impact, probable source, repair owner, and verification method.
